## 1. Purpose and disclosure

This log explains how generative AI was used as a support tool during the development of the Hospital OPD Booking System. AI assistance was **partial**. ChatGPT was used to support selected activities such as brainstorming, organizing information, clarifying concepts, reviewing wording, explaining technical topics, and suggesting refinements.

ChatGPT did not independently create the complete M1-M3 documents, make the team's project decisions, build and validate the entire system autonomously, or replace the team's design, implementation, and review work. The team remained responsible for the project scope, requirements, final wording, diagram decisions, prototype changes, presentation content, and repository submission.

AI-generated suggestions were treated as drafts or recommendations. The team reviewed them and decided whether to edit, use, or reject them before including them in the project.

## 5. Limitations and team responsibility

AI output can contain errors, omissions, or suggestions that do not fit the project. The team therefore did not treat ChatGPT responses as verified facts, formal approval, or a substitute for course instructions, source materials, or team decisions.

The team remains responsible for the submitted documents, diagrams, prototype, presentation, and repository contents. This log does not claim that the prototype is a production-ready clinical system or that every proposed feature has a completed backend implementation.

## 6. Team acknowledgement
